Beyond Vector Search

My ICLR 2026 workshop research explores hallucination-free financial reasoning with chunk-centric knowledge graphs: a retrieval path that can be followed and audited from entity to relationship to source chunk.

The throughline is inspection. Whether the system is reading filings, controlling a camera, or coordinating an agent loop, I care about making the reason for an answer visible enough that the next action can be trusted.
