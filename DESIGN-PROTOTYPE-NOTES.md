# World-lift design decision

**Chosen direction:** Variant E — Mechanical elevator / world lift.

**Status:** Formalized as the production homepage structure in `index.html`, with collection floors for projects, papers + research, field notes, and workshop experiments.

## Why it won

The site becomes a descent through Rithvik's actual operating loop rather than a generic project grid:

1. Arrival — what the site is for.
2. Listen — Agent Voice.
3. Look — HermesCamera.
4. Remember — agent-core and traces.
5. Recover — real-world failure handling.
6. Prove — research and evidence.
7. Workshop — experiments and contact.

The elevator is the viewing instrument, not the subject: a light structural canopy, narrow guide posts, one exposed drive/steam detail, a small brake console, and a floor indicator. The outside world and the work should remain the dominant image.

## Promotion constraints

- Rebuild E as the production route; do not promote `design-prototype.html` or its cumulative variant CSS directly.
- Keep a compact, keyboard-accessible floor navigation and preserve ordinary links/deep links alongside scroll storytelling.
- Use `prefers-reduced-motion` to make the lift stable while retaining all content.
- Keep the CC0 pipe, airship, and gear assets only after confirming final credits. Replace city background previews with owned/licensed production assets before launch.
- Keep the world coherent: fewer, larger physical details; the same copper/iron/paper palette; no decorative machinery that does not explain the lift.
